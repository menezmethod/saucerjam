# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

**Why this exists:** SaucerJam intends to move future releases to AGPL-3.0 and to keep the
option of dual licensing (AGPL-3.0 for the community, a commercial licence for companies
that want to embed, host or modify SaucerJam without the AGPL obligations). Neither is
possible unless the project holds more than a plain inbound=MIT grant from every
contributor. That is what this agreement provides, while leaving you the owner of your
own work.

This is the **Individual CLA** (§2–§6) and the **Corporate CLA** (§7). It is a licence
grant, **not** a copyright assignment: you keep ownership of your contribution.

> Not legal advice. This text should be reviewed by an attorney, alongside the wider
> review in [`docs/LEGAL-REVIEW.md`](docs/LEGAL-REVIEW.md), before it is relied on for a
> material commercial licence.

## 1. How you accept

You accept this agreement by either:

1. signing it through the CLA signing service wired to this repository (the intended
   mechanism — one signature covers all of your future pull requests), **or**
2. posting the exact sentence below as a comment on your pull request:

   > I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA.

A signature applies to the contribution in that pull request and to your future
contributions unless you withdraw it in writing for later work (§6.3). Maintainers may
decline a contribution that arrives without a signature. Signatures are recorded in the
pull request history and in the maintainer's CLA registry.

**Current status:** every commit in this repository so far is first-party work by the
project owner or by an agent acting under the owner's direction, so no outside signature
is on file yet and this agreement has not yet been exercised. The maintainer is the sole
copyright holder of everything currently in the tree. See
[`docs/LEGAL-REVIEW.md`](docs/LEGAL-REVIEW.md).

## 7. Corporate CLA

If you are contributing on behalf of a company or another legal entity, the same terms
apply with these changes, and an authorised representative must sign instead of the
individual:

- "I" means the entity, and the entity grants the licences in §2 and §3;
- the entity represents that it has the right to grant them and that each person
  submitting a contribution on its behalf is authorised to do so;
- the entity must list the GitHub usernames it covers, and update that list when
  contributors change;
- §4.2 applies to the entity's employment arrangements rather than to an individual's
  employer;
- the entity's withdrawal under §6.3 applies only to contributions not yet submitted.

## 8. Registry

Maintainers keep a private registry of signatures: name (or entity), GitHub username,
date, individual or corporate, and the pull request where the signature was recorded. A
copy of the acceptance comment lives in each pull request, which is the public record.
