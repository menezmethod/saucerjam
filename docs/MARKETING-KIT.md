## Launch sequence

1. Post the share banner with the short X / Bluesky copy.
2. Invite a small private group for a 6–8 player Jam Night.
3. Ask every first-time player for one confusion point and one memorable moment.
4. Post the strongest clip or player quote with the same play link.
5. Schedule the public session only after the private rehearsal is stable.
