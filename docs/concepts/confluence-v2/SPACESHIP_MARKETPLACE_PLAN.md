# Spaceship marketplace — deferred scope

The marketplace is a later product phase, not part of tile production or the first playable asset slice.

## Prerequisites

- validated ship GLB pipeline and provenance ledger;
- server-side cosmetic chassis ID with safe fallback;
- CDN caching and download limits;
- moderation/rejection for copyrighted or malicious uploads;
- fair fixed collision envelope and no gameplay stat changes;
- clear refunds, ownership, privacy, and creator-license terms.

## First marketplace version

Sell only cosmetic chassis and color/material variants that use the validated hardpoint contract. Do not add user-uploaded scripts, weapons, hitboxes, or physics. Keep payment, creator royalties, and physical printing behind a separate product decision after retention and asset demand are measured.

## Why the old mockup is not a build spec

The marketplace render is a visual concept. Prices, an 85/15 split, reviews, wallets, and 3D-print fulfillment are placeholders until legal, tax, storage, moderation, and operational requirements are designed and tested.
